# Living Room Ceiling Fan Lights Setup

## Goal

Add three new Living Room ceiling fan lights and expose them as a single, voice-controllable group, matching the office setup pattern but consolidated to one visible entity.

## Current State

- Three new ZHA lights are paired but unconfigured:
  - `light.third_reality_inc_3rcb01057z_4`
  - `light.third_reality_inc_3rcb01057z_5`
  - `light.third_reality_inc_3rcb01057z_6`
- The `Living Room` area already exists.
- The office uses three individually named/exposed fan lights plus a `light.ceiling_fan` group.

## Design

### Individual Lights

- Rename to:
  - `Living room ceiling fan light 1`
  - `Living room ceiling fan light 2`
  - `Living room ceiling fan light 3`
- Assign to the `Living Room` area (`living_room`).
- Hide from the default dashboard and disable voice exposure so only the group is visible/controllable.

### Group Light

- Create a `light` group helper named `Living room ceiling fan` (`light.living_room_ceiling_fan`).
- Members: the three individual lights above.
- Assign to the `Living Room` area.
- Expose to the voice assistant with aliases such as `living room ceiling fan`.

### Dashboard

The dashboard is auto-generated by `.dashboard-gen/build-dashboards.js`. Once the group light exists and the 3 individual bulbs are hidden, the new `light.living_room_ceiling_fan` will appear automatically in the Lights view. The 3 individual bulb entity IDs must be added to `EXCLUDED_LIGHTS` in `build-dashboards.js` so they don't render as separate tiles.

### Scenes

Add to `scenes.yaml`:

- `living_room_focus_mode` — group on at 100% brightness.
- `living_room_relax_mode` — group on at 30% brightness.
- `living_room_all_lights_off` — group off.

### Scripts

Add to `scripts.yaml`:

- `living_room_lights_on` — turn the group on at 100% brightness.
- `living_room_lights_off` — turn the group off.

## Files to Change

- `configuration.yaml` — add the `light:` group definition.
- `scenes.yaml` — add the three scenes.
- `scripts.yaml` — add the two scripts.
- `.dashboard-gen/build-dashboards.js` — add the 3 individual light entity IDs to `EXCLUDED_LIGHTS` so only the group shows on the auto-generated Lights dashboard.
- `home-assistant_v2.db` / entity registry — update names, area, hidden status, and voice exposure via HA config entries (done through YAML/helpers + UI state where applicable).

## Success Criteria

- Only `light.living_room_ceiling_fan` is visible in the UI and voice assistant.
- The group controls all three fan lights together.
- Scenes and scripts for the Living Room work independently of the office.
- HA config check passes with no errors.
